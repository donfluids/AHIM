About Our Church

Aron HaBrit International Ministries was established in Laurel on October 10, 2016, as a revival church. Families from many backgrounds have made it their home in the years since.

Preaching is in Malayalam and English, and we sing in Tamil and Hindi as well. The children have Sunday School and an hour of their own during the service; everyone else worships together, one generation beside the next.

Our Name

“Aron HaBrit” is Hebrew for the Ark of the Covenant, the place where God's presence dwelt among His people. It reflects our desire to be a church built around the presence of God.

“Let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them.” Exodus 25:8

Our Pastor

Pastor Monish Stephen · Senior Pastor

Pastor Monish Stephen leads Aron HaBrit International Ministries with a prophetic calling. His ministry is marked by healing, prophecy, and the interpretation of dreams and visions, and he preaches in Malayalam and English at our Sunday services and special meetings.

Through the year, guest ministers such as Prophet Tijo Thomas and Pastor Shajan George join him for conferences and revival services.
